# v5 Childcare

## Sketch
- Post a sitter request, offer to sit
- Babysitting hours ledger between households (no money)
- Store almost nothing about children: first name and age range at most, visible only to members the parent chooses. No photos of children.

Regular shared care (rotating hosts) is blocked until PA licensing is checked. A home caring for 4 to 6 unrelated children needs a license, paid or not.
