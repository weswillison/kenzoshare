# v2 Grocery Share

## Goal
Cut individual grocery trips from weekly to about twice a month.

## Must have
- Schedule a run: store (Costco, Aldi, Lidl), date, runner, order cutoff
- Costco: list an item split into shares, members claim shares, item locks when fully claimed, unclaimed items drop at cutoff
- Aldi/Lidl: each household adds items to a shared list for the run
- Per-household total including runner fee ($3 per household, on top of groceries and tax)
- Mark paid (payment happens off-site) and delivered

## Not in scope
Prices pulled from store sites. In-app payments.

## Open
- Teen runners: deferred until after the pilot check-in. Adult runners only for now.
