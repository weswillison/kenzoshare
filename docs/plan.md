# KenzoShare Plan

kenzoshare.org: a sharing site for neighbors, starting with a small pilot on one block in Kensington. Free, no ads, members only, open source.

## Phase 1: Garage Share
Things that would live in a garage or basement if we had one: tools, ladders, paddle boards, camping gear.
- Items stay with their owners and are listed on the site.
- Each item lists a replacement cost, so borrowers know what they'd owe if it's lost or broken.
- Borrowing, returning, and handing off to another member are marked on the site.
- You can set a return date when you borrow, but you don't have to.
- Books, games, and puzzles get added once the first category is working.

## Phase 2: Grocery Share
Goal: cut individual grocery trips from weekly to about twice a month.
- Costco: members claim portions of bulk items. An item is bought once it's fully claimed.
- Aldi/Lidl: one person shops for several households from a shared list.
- Members pay their share upfront.
- Whoever does the run delivers and gets $3 from each household on the run, on top of groceries and tax. We'll see how that works and adjust.
- Could become a paid job for a teen driver later.

## Later
- Car share (Honda Fit). Pending insurance.
- Meal sharing. Weekly cooking rotation: who cooks, how many plates, dietary needs and safe foods. Already happening between two households.
- Childcare. Sitter requests and babysitting hour swaps. Regular shared care pending a check of PA licensing rules (a home caring for 4 to 6 unrelated kids needs a license, paid or not).
- Streaming logins: maybe, if feasible. Most services make it difficult.

## How it works
- One login per person.
- Profiles show contact info and borrowing and lending history.
- Only members can see the site.
- Invite-only during the pilot. Any member can invite a neighbor, and the site remembers who invited whom. We'll start with up to 12 households and talk before growing past that.
- No payments through the site. Venmo, Cash App, or cash.
- Wes builds and maintains it, Preston helps. Code and plans live in a shared repo.

## Ground rules
- Return items clean and in the same condition.
- Report breakage or loss to the owner and resolve it together. The listed replacement cost is the starting point.
- Lend only what you could live without. During the pilot there's no shared fund to cover a loss. Owner and borrower work it out between them.
- Update the site when an item changes hands.
- Lending is optional.

## Timeline
1. Site setup. Collect lists of garage-share items.
2. Garage Share trial, about 2 months.
3. First shared grocery run.
4. Check-in meeting.
